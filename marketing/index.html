( 01 · FOCUS )

The 15-minute article that took 31. Clem catches it.

Screen-time apps give you totals. Clem reconstructs your attention — every app, every browser tab, in order — and shows exactly where it broke, what pulled you away, and what to change tomorrow.

  • Task reports, not app totals. Expected vs. actual time, minute-by-minute timeline, attention score per task.
  • Habit detection across days. “You open X within the first 5 minutes of every long read.” Nobody else knows that about you.
  • Focus sessions. Lock one thing. Drift for a minute, and a nudge lands over whatever you switched to — anywhere on the Mac.
  • Eyes on screen. On-device webcam checks tell watching from wandering when you go idle. No frames stored, ever.

( 02 · MEMORY )

Chat with everything you’ve ever seen.

Clem quietly watches your screen, reads it with on-device OCR, and turns your day into a searchable memory. Ask “what was that error I saw an hour ago?” — get a synthesized answer with citations to the actual moments. Summon it from anywhere with ⌥Space.

  • Ground truth, not self-reporting. A persistent trace of everything you searched, opened and worked on.
  • The daily briefing. An auto-written recap of your day — plus every loose end you dropped.
  • Save deliberately. ⌥S captures whatever’s on screen as a memory, with context attached.
  • It forgets on command. Wipe any time range. Blocked apps and sites never enter memory at all.
( 03 · DISCOVER )

A feed of your own curiosity.

One-click connectors import your entire digital past — Chrome and Brave bookmarks, X bookmarks, YouTube playlists and history — into a feed ranked by what you’ve actually been consuming lately. Your 2020 bookmarks become tonight’s feed. You are the algorithm.

( THE DEAL )

This invasive — because nothing leaves your Mac.

A tool that watches your screen, your habits, and your gaze should answer to exactly one person. Clem runs entirely on localhost — the fire stays with you.

⌂ Runs on localhost

No cloud, no account, no telemetry. The memory engine, the OCR, the models — all on your machine.

◉ On-device everything

Screen text is read on-device. Webcam checks are analysed in memory and discarded — no frames stored, no keystrokes logged.

⌫ Forgetting built in

Block apps and sites from ever being seen. Delete any time range. Export everything. It’s your mind — you hold the keys.
